Not equipment. Sadler's chapter is a list of proper names: kettles called after the places they came from, after a peculiarity of their shape, and sometimes comically: Raisei, thunder-voice; Ubaguchi, a hag's mouth. The trivet that holds the kettle is called Gotoku, the five virtues. Each of these has one task, a name, and often a history longer than the house it is used in.
