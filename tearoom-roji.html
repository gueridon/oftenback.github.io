露地roji

The dewy path. Rikyu took the name from the Lotus Sutra, where those who escape the burning house take their seats on dewy ground.

It is not a garden to be admired: it is a passage, and nothing in it is allowed to be remarkable. Ordinary trees, valueless stones, moss. Asked to describe the ideal one, Rikyu answered only thick green moss, all pure and sunny warm.

It is two gardens rather than one. An outer, sanded, where guests wait; an inner, mossy, past the middle gate. Crossing that gate is meant to feel like a change of scene. It ends at a door too low to pass standing.
