Thin tea over the sunken hearth, the procedure this room plays, step by step. 73 steps: the four hands that carry the utensils in, the six that set them down, and the sixty beats of the ceremony itself, 13 minutes of movement in all. Each number is a door, and opens the room at that moment.

The steps and their order are the standard ones, and this is not a second account of them: the page and the room read the same list, so what stands here is exactly what plays there, step for step and in the same order. The wording is ours.

Opening words

  1. What follows is one moment of one procedure, in our own room. The host brings in what is needed a hand at a time: the sweets first, then the water, then the bowl with its whisk, scoop and cloth, and last the waste water. Each thing is set where it belongs, and names itself as it arrives.

Four hands, in this order. Every piece names itself as it arrives and none of them is described.
