茶室 chashitsu  ·  the eight hearths

A tea room is classified by where the hearth is cut, not only by how many mats it holds. Four positions, each in a normal and a reversed style, make the eight. Everything else follows from it: where the host kneels, which way the guests sit, which corner of the floor is opened.

tokonoma
nijiriguchi

  • 炉 ro, the hearth
  • 点前座 temae-za, the host's mat
  • 亭主 the host
  • 客 the guests
  • 中柱 naka-bashira, in the daime only

A. L. Sadler, Cha-No-Yu: The Japanese Tea Ceremony, on the varieties of tearoom.
